motion planner pipeline:
    1) gait selection, sort into tripods
    2) velocity target
    3) foot velocities in body frame
    4) compute foot delta for cycle (dP_i)
    5) compute p_swing = p_idle + dP_i/2, p_stance = p_idle - dP_i/2 (linear advance dP_i per leg movement/half cycle/stance phase)
    6) compute p_lift = p(s) = (1 - s)*p_rear + s*p_front ??? and z_lift = z_ground + h*
    7) gait scheduler...
